The old train station sat quietly beneath a blanket of pale morning fog. Maya clutched the leather handle of her suitcase, watching the damp tracks stretch into the distance. She had waited three long years for this exact moment. A distant whistle cut through the chill, low and steady, making her heart skip a beat. The heavy iron doors groaned open with a hiss of steam. She stepped forward, leaving the past behind her on the frost-bitten platform. Every breath felt lighter now. The journey had finally begun, and the world was waiting just around the bend.
